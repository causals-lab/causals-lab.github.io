Why this exists

A graph should explain its answers.

A line between two things can mean many things: ownership, a supply relationship, an observed association, or a causal claim. Treating them all as the same would produce a compelling picture—and a misleading answer.

Our approach keeps the type, direction, provenance, time context, and uncertainty of each relationship visible. The graph is a place to investigate a hypothesis, not a machine that turns every connection into proof.

How reasoning earns trust

Evidence has layers.

A plausible path and a past pattern are different kinds of evidence. We want people to see both—and the misses.
