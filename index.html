The mission

A graph that can be questioned.

Real-world causes are rarely simple. A useful graph must preserve where a claim came from, when it applies, what evidence supports it, and where people disagree. We aim to make those distinctions inspectable—not hide them behind a confident answer.

The work

Four connected efforts

01

Causals Graph

An evolving, reviewable map of entities, relationships, evidence, and uncertainty.

02

Causals Ontology

A shared language for expressing what a relationship means—and what it does not.

03

Causals Bench

Evaluation tasks that test whether graph-and-LLM reasoning is grounded and useful.

04

Causals Commons

A place for people to propose, investigate, review, and challenge the graph.
