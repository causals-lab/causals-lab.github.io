A useful graph proposal

Give others enough to verify.

  1. Identify the record. Include its stable ID or link if one exists, and describe the exact change.
  2. Show the evidence. Link to accessible sources and explain what each actually supports.
  3. State the limits. Note scope, time period, uncertainty, and known counterevidence.
  4. Expect review. A proposal may be accepted, disputed, revised, or declined with a reason.
Contribution principle
Source material, a graph record, and an interpretation of causality are separate things. Please make the distinction explicit in your proposal.

Link to sources instead of copying substantial copyrighted material. Do not post non-public personal information. For full guidance, read the contribution guide ↗.
