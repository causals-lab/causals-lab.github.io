What the graph holds

More than arrows.

Some relationships describe physical flows, such as production or supply. Others describe capital and obligations. Some record where a person works or where an entity is located. Events and their reported effects form another layer.

Only relationships with an explicit transmission meaning should participate in a propagation calculation. A descriptive edge remains useful for search and context without becoming a causal shortcut.

Important distinction
A graph path suggests a mechanism worth investigating. It does not establish that an intervention would cause the same result, or that an asset will move in a predicted direction.

How to examine an answer

Ask three questions.

01 / MEANING

What does each edge mean?

Check direction, relationship type, scope, and whether the link is descriptive or part of a proposed transmission route.

02 / EVIDENCE

What supports it?

Follow sources, provenance, time context, and counterevidence. A cited statement and a measured effect are not interchangeable.

03 / VALIDATION

What happened before?

Where historical testing exists, examine the sample and misses alongside any summary statistic.
